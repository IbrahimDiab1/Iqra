Folio, round two

Updated from your 10 comments. Everything is clickable; books, notes and numbers are sample data.

What changed

  • 1Select any words. Tap a word to select it. To select a range, drag with the mouse on PC, or long-press and drag on your phone.
  • 2No more “time left”. Progress is in pages, with time spent shown second.
  • 3Page view by default, the PDF page as printed. The icon next to the frame switches to Reading mode: the same text, larger and reflowed. Your highlights show in both views.
  • 4–5Status and category filter together. “Continue reading” shows the most recent book in whatever you’ve filtered.
  • 6, 10Settings (the gear in Library): your Drive and device storage, offline copies, categories, defaults and export.
  • 7Weekly goals, as many as you want, each by category, in time or pages. The daily streak is gone.
  • 8Flashcards both ways: English → Arabic, Arabic → English, or mixed.
  • 9Tap highlighted text to read its note right on the page. A small dot marks highlights that have a note.
  • 10Edit book details: long-press a book (right-click on PC), or use the pencil in Contents & notes.

The sample book is the public-domain 1859 text of On the Origin of Species, pages 11–12. For the demo, only a few words and phrases have their own Arabic translation; any other selection shows the full sentence’s translation. The real app translates exactly what you select.
